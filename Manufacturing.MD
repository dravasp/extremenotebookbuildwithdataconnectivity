# Manufacturing Documentation

## Product Portfolio

### Solid Carbide Tools
- **Step Drills** → Precision drilling for PCB vias and microvias.
- **End Mills** → CNC milling of aluminum chassis and heatsink slots.
- **Custom Carbide Solutions** → Specialized cuts for connector housings and custom geometries.

### PCD Tools (Poly‑crystalline Diamond)
- **High Wear Resistance** → Machining aerospace aluminum and composite panels.
- **Reduced Tooling Cost** → Long life cycles for repetitive chassis milling.
- **Applications** → MicroLED display frames, precision slots.

### CBN Tools (Cubic Boron Nitride)
- **Rough Turning** → Hardened alloy brackets and hinge assemblies.
- **Finish Milling** → Titanium hinge components, vibration‑free cuts.
- **Applications** → GPU/NPU heatsink mounts, aerospace‑grade fasteners.

### Carbide Tip Brazed Tools
- **Angle Cutters** → Precision slotting for connector cages (QSFP-DD, USB-C).
- **Boring/Facing Tools** → Internal bore finishing for DC jacks and audio ports.
- **Reamers** → Tight tolerance finishing of screw holes and alignment pins.

### HSS Tools (High-Speed Steel)
- **Economical Machining** → Prototype builds and low-volume parts.
- **Versatile Applications** → General drilling, tapping, and assembly fixtures.

### Aerospace Tooling
- **Frames** → CNC machining of notebook chassis with aerospace aluminum 7075-T6.
- **Engine Parts** → Adapted for blower assemblies and thermal modules.
- **Compliance** → MIL-STD-810G vibration/shock validation.

### Tool Holders
- **Precision Holders** → CNC spindle stability, reduced chatter.
- **Manual Machining** → Serviceability and rework operations.
- **Applications** → PCB drilling, connector slotting, chassis finishing.
